CASE STUDY OF THE DAY
TRIAGE-CPG: A Cross-Model Study of Budgeted Static Malware Triage with Large Language Models
The study shows that large language models can triage unknown Windows binaries by analysing only a small set of functions, achieving up to 85% balanced accuracy while flagging most malware and clearing most benign samples. They built a fixed pipeline (TRIAGE-CPG) that selects seed functions, expands evidence under a budget, and outputs a verdict with a behavioural report.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21331v1
