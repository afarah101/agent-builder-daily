How we use your email

Daily Edge AI · Last updated 27 Nov 2026

Who we are

Daily Edge AI is run by Axiom Tech LTD, trading as Optomate. Axiom Tech LTD is the data controller.

Contact: admin@optomate.co.uk

What we collect and why

Only your email address, and only if you sign up with the form on the site.

We use it to send you the Daily Edge AI email: AI news and real case studies, every day. We do not sell your email, share it with advertisers or use it for anything else.

Our lawful basis under the UK GDPR is your consent. You give it when you tick the box on the sign-up form.

Where it is kept

Google is our data processor. The sign-up form sends your email to a Google Apps Script, and it is stored in our Google account.

Google may process data outside the UK. Google protects these transfers with the safeguards in its data processing terms, including the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. Email us for a copy.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until you unsubscribe. Then we delete it within 7 days.

We keep a record of your consent with your email: the date, the time and the words you agreed to.

How to unsubscribe

  • Click "Unsubscribe" at the foot of any Daily Edge AI email, or
  • Email admin@optomate.co.uk with the subject "Unsubscribe Daily Edge AI".

This withdraws your consent. You can do it at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how we use it, to receive it in a portable form (data portability), and to withdraw your consent.

Right to object: you can object to how we use your data at any time. Tell us and we stop using it for that purpose. You do not have to give a reason.

To use a right, email admin@optomate.co.uk.

How to complain

You have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk.
