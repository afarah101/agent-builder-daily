Scout Daily · last updated 26 November 2026

What we collect

Only your email address, and only if you sign up with the form on the site.

Why we collect it

To send you the Scout Daily email: AI agent case studies and news for IT engineers. We do not sell your email, share it with advertisers or use it for anything else.

Where it is kept

The sign-up form sends it to a Google Apps Script run by the Scout Daily team, which stores it in our Google account. It stays there until you unsubscribe.

How to unsubscribe

  • Click "Unsubscribe" at the foot of any Scout Daily email, or
  • Email admin@optomate.co.uk with the subject "Unsubscribe Scout Daily".

We remove your email within 7 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the email we hold for you at any time.
