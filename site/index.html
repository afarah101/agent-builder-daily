Agent in a Bottle: Can LLM Agents Turn Their Capabilities Into Cheap, Scalable Artifacts?

Ankit Sonthalia, Haritz Puerto, Alexander Rubinstein, Martin Gubri · Academic Institution · 06 Oct 2026

The paper explores how LLM agents can ‘bottle’ their general skills into custom, cheaper models for large batches of similar tasks, reducing run‑time costs while keeping quality. They built BOTTLED, a benchmark that lets agents decide whether to fine‑tune a small model, chain prompts, or other strategies, and showed significant savings on a synthetic workload. This is relevant for cloud managers looking to optimise LLM‑driven workloads and cut API spend.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

If you manage cloud LLM services, step 1: set up a BOTTLED‑style test by feeding your workload into an agent; step 2: let the agent choose a low‑cost model or prompt strategy; step 3: deploy the resulting bottled model in your environment and monitor cost vs. quality.
