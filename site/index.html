QuoteBench: How Matched Scores Can Hide Command-Path Failures

Shangao Li, Yao Zhang, Volker Tresp, Yuanyuan Yang · NYU · 13 Aug 2026

This research solves the problem of hidden command-path failures in large language models, the team built QuoteBench to measure these failures, and the result shows that matched execution scores are not enough to distinguish errors. The research aims to improve the reliability of LLM coding agents. It focuses on the intersection of code generation and execution.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

If you manage LLM-powered coding tools, check your command execution pipeline for potential failures that may not be caught by matched execution scores alone, and consider implementing exact final-state validation to ensure reliability. This flags a risk if your organization relies on LLMs for automation or scripting tasks. Not directly applicable to general cloud infrastructure management.
