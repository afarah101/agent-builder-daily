Trajectory-Level Speculation with Semantic Verification for Tool-Using Agents

Zilin Wang, Yinhong Liu, Chao Chen, Qun Liu · Academic Institution · 15 Oct 2026

SpecAgent is a new way for large‑language‑model agents to split work: a big, powerful model writes the overall plan, while a smaller model does most routine tool calls and the big model only steps in to check the smaller model’s output. The authors built a collaboration protocol that lets the small model draft many steps before the big model reviews them, reducing the heavy model’s workload. The result is lower compute usage for complex task chains, with no loss of accuracy in the final action sequence.
