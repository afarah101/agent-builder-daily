BI-Agent and BI-Bench: Towards Automating End-to-End Business Intelligence

Chuxuan Hu, Yeye He, Penny Zhou, Wee Hyong Tok · Academic Institution · 16 Sep 2026

BI‑Agent uses large language models to automatically identify tables, transform data, and build joins needed to answer business questions, eliminating the manual steps in traditional BI. The authors created a benchmark (BI‑Bench) to evaluate such systems, showing that LLM‑driven workflows can match or exceed human performance on common BI tasks. This shows a way to reduce data‑prep effort in BI tools like Power BI and Tableau.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

If you manage cloud data services, here is what to do: 1) check if your BI platform can integrate LLM APIs for automated table discovery and transformations; 2) pilot the LLM‑driven workflow on a small dataset to gauge accuracy; 3) monitor performance and adjust compute resources to handle the extra inference load.
