GraphMind: From Operational Traces to Self-Evolving Workflow Automation

Yiwen Zhu, Joyce Cahoon, Anna Pavlenko, Qiushi Bai · Academic Institution · 17 May 2026

GraphMind solves the problem of automating complex operational workflows by building a system that constructs, executes, and evolves action-centric workflow graphs with minimal human effort. The researchers built a system that extracts workflow graphs from operational traces and adapts over time. The result is a self-evolving workflow automation system that can automate tasks with minimal human input.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

If you manage workflow automation, here is what to do: review your current automation systems to see if they can be improved with GraphMind's self-evolving workflow graphs, assess the potential for adapting GraphMind to your organisation's specific workflow needs, and consider implementing GraphMind to reduce manual effort and improve automation efficiency.
