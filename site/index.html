Agent Evaluation Reliability: More Tasks Won't (Always) Fix An Agent Leaderboard

Michael Hardy, Ruhana Azam, Anka Reuel, Mykel Kochenderfer · Academic Institution · 30 Sep 2026

The paper shows that rankings of AI agents can change depending on the specific tasks and conditions used in testing, meaning current leaderboards may not truly reflect an agent’s overall performance. The authors built a Bayesian model to separate out how much of the ranking variance comes from the model itself versus the evaluation setup, applied it to 22 benchmarks, and found that adding more tasks often doesn't fix ranking reliability. This is not relevant to everyday cloud infrastructure management.
