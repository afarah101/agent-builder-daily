Learning to Steer LLM Agents and Multi-Agent Systems

Leyang Shen, Yaxin Gong, Yang Zhang, Chenxiao Fan · Academic Institution · 15 Oct 2026

Goal drift in large‑language‑model agents means they can stray from their overall objective during long‑run tasks, wasting resources and producing wrong outputs. The authors built a lightweight steering agent that watches an agent’s actions in real time, deciding when to step in with a quick corrective move to bring it back on track. The result is fewer missed goals and more efficient use of computation in multi‑agent setups.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

If you manage LLM‑powered services, implement a monitoring layer that tracks agent decisions; when the context shows a deviation, trigger a low‑cost corrective routine, and log the intervention for future training. Check that your orchestration platform can inject such a steering agent without breaking existing pipelines.
