Operating-Layer Controls for Onchain Language-Model Agents Under Real Capital

T. J. Barton, Chris Constantakis, Patti Hauseman, Annie Mous · Academic Institution · 28 Apr 2026

The problem of reliability in autonomous language-model agents is addressed in this study, which explores the use of operating-layer controls to translate user mandates into validated tool actions under real capital. The AI approach involves the deployment of 3,505 user-funded agents in a bounded onchain market, with users configuring vaults through structured controls and natural-language strategies. The outcome is a system that produced 7.5M agent invocations, roughly 300K onchain actions, and a 99.9% settlement success rate for policy validity.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

To apply this approach, AI practitioners can focus on designing operating-layer controls that allow users to configure and interact with autonomous agents in a secure and reliable manner. By leveraging natural-language strategies and structured controls, practitioners can build more effective and scalable AI systems that can operate in real-world environments.

BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY

The study demonstrates a clear business opportunity for companies to develop and deploy autonomous language-model agents that can operate in onchain markets, with potential applications in areas such as cryptocurrency trading and asset management. By building on the findings of this study, companies can create products and services that provide reliable and secure autonomous trading capabilities, with potential for significant revenue generation.
