Algospeak, Hiding in the Open: The Trade-off Between Legible Meaning and Detection Avoidance

Jan Fillies, Ronald E. Robertson, Jeffrey Hancock · Academic Institution · 07 May 2026

This research solves the problem of linguistic evasion strategies in large language models, introducing the concept of Majority Understandable Modulation, and finds that as evasion increases, detectability and understandability decrease. The authors built a joint action model to formalize the dynamics between evaders and detectors. The result shows a trade-off between hiding from detectors and being understood by recipients.
