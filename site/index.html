Characterizing Vision-Language-Action Models across XPUs: Constraints and Acceleration for On-Robot Deployment

Kaijun Zhou, Qiwei Chen, Da Peng, Zhiyang Li · Academic Institution · 27 Apr 2026

The problem of real-time inference in Vision-Language-Action models for on-robot deployment is addressed through a systematic analysis of model-hardware co-characterization. The AI approach involves building a cross-accelerator leaderboard to evaluate model-hardware pairs under various constraints, leading to the identification of right-sized edge devices for low-cost deployment. The outcome is a more efficient and cost-effective solution for VLA model deployment on robots.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

To apply this approach, AI practitioners can utilize the cross-accelerator leaderboard to evaluate and select the most suitable model-hardware pairs for their specific use case, considering factors such as cost, energy, and time. By doing so, they can optimize their VLA model deployment for on-robot applications and achieve better performance and efficiency.

BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY

The research presents a concrete business opportunity for companies developing robot control systems, as the optimized VLA model deployment can lead to significant cost savings and improved performance. This can be particularly relevant for industries such as robotics, manufacturing, and logistics, where autonomous robots are increasingly being used, and efficient control systems are crucial for their operation.

AgentPulse: A Continuous Multi-Signal Framework for Evaluating AI Agents in Deployment

Yuxuan Gao, Megan Wang, Yi Ling Yu · Academic Institution · 27 Apr 2026

The current static benchmarks for evaluating AI agents have limitations as they only measure performance at a fixed point in time, neglecting how these agents are adopted, maintained, or experienced during deployment. The AgentPulse framework addresses this issue by continuously evaluating AI agents across multiple signals and factors, providing a more comprehensive assessment. By doing so, it captures a broader range of information, including adoption, community sentiment, and ecosystem health, thereby offering a more nuanced understanding of AI agent deployment.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

AI practitioners can apply the lessons from AgentPulse by incorporating continuous evaluation into their agent development workflows, utilizing real-time data from various sources to monitor and improve agent performance in deployment. This approach allows for more adaptive and responsive AI systems that can better meet the evolving needs of users and environments.

BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY

The AgentPulse framework presents a concrete business opportunity in the form of a subscription-based service that offers continuous evaluation and monitoring of AI agents for companies, providing them with actionable insights to enhance their AI-driven products and services. By leveraging the comprehensive assessment provided by AgentPulse, businesses can improve the reliability, efficiency, and overall value of their AI investments, leading to potential revenue growth and competitive advantage.

Network Impact of Post-Quantum Certificate Chain sizes on Time to First Byte in TLS Deployments

Matthew Chou, Phuong Cao · Academic Institution · 27 Apr 2026

The problem of transitioning existing internet infrastructure to quantum-safe certificates is a pressing concern due to the vulnerability of traditional cryptographic algorithms to quantum computer attacks. The authors evaluate the time to first byte under CDN-focused TLS conditions to characterize the latency cost of this transition, using post-quantum cryptography (PQC) to assess the impact on network performance. The outcome of this study provides insights into the deployment challenges of PQC in real-world networks, highlighting the significant increase in handshake sizes compared to classical algorithms.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

To apply the findings of this study, AI practitioners should consider the latency cost of transitioning to quantum-safe certificates and evaluate the impact on their network performance, taking into account the increased handshake sizes and potential deployment challenges. By doing so, they can develop strategies to mitigate the effects of PQC on their systems and ensure a smooth transition to quantum-safe cryptography.

BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY

The findings of this study present a business opportunity for companies to develop and provide solutions that can efficiently deploy and manage quantum-safe certificates, reducing the latency cost and minimizing the impact on network performance. By offering such solutions, companies can help organizations transition to PQC and stay ahead of the quantum threat, creating a new revenue stream and establishing themselves as leaders in the field of quantum-safe cryptography.

Feasible-First Exploration for Constrained ML Deployment Optimization in Crash-Prone Hierarchical Search Spaces

Christian Lysenstøen · Academic Institution · 27 Apr 2026

The problem of deploying machine learning models under production constraints is addressed, where many configurations are invalid due to crashes, memory limits, or latency constraints. The proposed AI approach, Feasible-First Exploration, aims to optimize the deployment of machine learning models in hierarchical search spaces with invalid configurations. The outcome of this approach is to reduce the evaluation budget spent on invalid configurations and improve the overall optimization process.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

AI practitioners can apply this approach by first identifying the feasible configurations in the search space and then performing optimization, rather than using standard black-box optimizers that may waste evaluations on invalid configurations. By doing so, practitioners can improve the efficiency of their machine learning model deployment process.

BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY

The proposed approach can be monetized by developing a product that automates the deployment optimization process for machine learning models, targeting companies that require efficient and reliable model deployment. This product can be sold as a software solution or a service, offering customization and support for various production environments and model types.
