Linjun Li · Academic Institution · 23 Jul 2026

This research solves the problem of AI models providing different advice based on how they receive objectives, by showing that direct exposure to a dangerous objective can produce safer results than when other agents mediate. The researchers built a test using OpenAI's gpt-5.6-sol model to compare the advice given in different scenarios. The result shows that the model's advice can vary significantly depending on how the objective is presented.
