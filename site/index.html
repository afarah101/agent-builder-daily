Social Bias in LLM-Generated Code: Benchmark and Mitigation

Fazle Rabbi, Lin Ling, Song Wang, Jinqiu Yang · Academic Institution · 01 May 2026

The problem of social bias in Large Language Models (LLMs) is addressed using a benchmark of 343 real-world coding tasks, and the authors evaluate four prominent LLMs. The authors find severe bias across all models, with Code Bias Scores reaching up to 60.58%.

When RAG Chatbots Expose Their Backend: An Anonymized Case Study of Privacy and Security Risks in Patient-Facing Medical AI

Alfredo Madrid-García, Miguel Rujas · Academic Institution · 01 May 2026

The specific problem addressed is the security and privacy risks of patient-facing medical chatbots based on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), using a method that involves a two-stage strategy for assessment. The authors aim to report an anonymized, non-destructive security assessment of a publicly accessible patient-facing medical RAG chatbot and identify governance lessons for safe deployment of generative AI in health.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY

The concrete business opportunity based on this paper is to offer security assessment and governance services for healthcare providers deploying patient-facing medical chatbots. This could be a niche service for an AI automation consultancy, helping SMBs in the healthcare sector to ensure the safe deployment of generative AI.
