Preserving Utility in Continual LLM Unlearning via Orthogonal Projections

Juan Belieni, Ana Carolina Erthal, Eliezer de Souza da Silva, Diego Mesquita·29 Oct 2026

This research tackles the problem of repeatedly removing sensitive data from a large language model without losing performance. The authors built Continual Orthogonal Projection (COP), a simple gradient‑projection method that keeps each deletion orthogonal to previous ones, so utility stays high and forgetting stays strong. Experiments on standard benchmarks show COP outperforms existing continual‑unlearning techniques.

How to apply: If you manage cloud‑hosted LLMs, here is what to do: 1) add COP’s low‑rank basis cache to your inference pipeline; 2) whenever a deletion request arrives, run a single gradient step constrained to COP’s orthogonal subspace; 3) monitor utility metrics to confirm no drift. If your org uses continual data deletion, check that your current unlearning routine isn’t causing interference between successive requests.
