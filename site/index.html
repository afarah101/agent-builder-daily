Harry Mayne, Lev McKinney, Jan Dubiński, Adam Karvonen · Academic Institution · 13 May 2026

This research identifies a problem where AI models can fail to learn negations in training, leading to incorrect beliefs. The researchers built models that were finetuned on documents with false claims and found that the models believed the claims to be true. The result shows a significant increase in incorrect beliefs, from 2.5% to 88%.

HOW TO APPLY THIS
