Substrate Asymmetry in User-Side Memory: A Diagnostic Framework

Youwang Deng · Academic Institution · 10 Jun 2026

This research solves the problem of evaluating user-side memory in large language models by breaking it down into three orthogonal axes, creating a diagnostic framework to identify opposite-direction failures.
