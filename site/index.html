SynSur: An end-to-end generative pipeline for synthetic industrial surface defect generation and detection

Paul Julius Kühn, Mika Pommeranz, Arjan Kuijper, Saptarshi Neil Sinha · Academic Institution · 29 Apr 2026

The scarcity of labeled defect data hinders the development of learning-based industrial defect detection systems. To address this, the proposed SynSur pipeline generates synthetic defects using a combination of Vision-Language-Model-based prompts, LoRA-adapted diffusion, and mask-guided inpainting. The evaluation demonstrates the potential of using real data with realistic synthetic samples to overcome data scarcity and improve defect detection accuracy.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

AI practitioners can apply the SynSur pipeline by integrating it into their existing defect detection systems, using the generated synthetic defects to augment their training data and improve model performance. Additionally, they can fine-tune the pipeline's parameters to adapt to their specific use cases and defect types.

BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY

The SynSur pipeline presents a concrete business opportunity for companies developing industrial defect detection systems, as it can help reduce the costs and time associated with data collection and annotation.
