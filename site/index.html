DeltaBox: Scaling Stateful AI Agents with Millisecond-Level Sandbox Checkpoint/Rollback

Yunpeng Dong, Jingkai He, Yuze Hou, Dong Du · Academic Institution · 21 May 2026

This research solves the problem of slow checkpoint and rollback in AI agents by building DeltaBox, a system that scales stateful AI agents with millisecond-level sandbox checkpoint and rollback, resulting in significantly reduced latency. DeltaBox achieves this by only duplicating the changes between checkpoints, rather than the entire state. This breakthrough enables faster and more efficient AI agent training and deployment.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

If you manage cloud infrastructure for AI workloads, here is what to do: review your current checkpoint and rollback processes for potential bottlenecks, consider implementing DeltaBox or similar delta-based checkpointing systems, and evaluate the potential performance gains for your specific use cases.
