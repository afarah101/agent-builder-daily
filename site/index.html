Compute Where it Counts: Self Optimizing Language Models

Yash Akhauri, Mohamed S. Abdelfattah · Academic Institution · 11 May 2026

This research solves the problem of inefficient computation in language models by dynamically allocating computation budget to each token, rather than applying a uniform budget. The researchers built a Self-Optimizing Language Model (SOL) that pairs a language model with a policy network to learn how much computation to spend per token. The result is a more efficient language model that can adapt to varying token difficulty.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

If you manage language model infrastructure, here is what to do: review your current language model deployment for opportunities to implement dynamic computation budget allocation, assess the potential benefits of integrating a policy network with your existing language model, and consider experimenting with SOL to improve efficiency and reduce costs.
