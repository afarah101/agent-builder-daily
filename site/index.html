TRIAGE-CPG: A Cross-Model Study of Budgeted Static Malware Triage with Large Language Models

Paul-Andrei Sava, Alexander Küchler, Daniel Kowatsch, Bernhard Grill·16 Nov 2026

The study shows that large language models can triage unknown Windows binaries by analysing only a small set of functions, achieving up to 85% balanced accuracy while flagging most malware and clearing most benign samples. They built a fixed pipeline (TRIAGE-CPG) that selects seed functions, expands evidence under a budget, and outputs a verdict with a behavioural report. This research is not directly relevant to routine cloud infrastructure management.

The paper proposes a practical pipeline for malware triage using LLMs, directly impacting how security engineers assess binaries. The approach could be adopted quickly as a service or integrated into existing SOC workflows, offering tangible monetisation potential for IT infrastructure teams.
