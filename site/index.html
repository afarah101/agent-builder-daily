LLMSurgeon: Diagnosing Data Mixture of Large Language Models

Yaxin Luo, Jiacheng Cui, Xiaohan Zhao, Xinyi Shang · Academic Institution · 28 May 2026

This research solves the problem of identifying the pretraining data mixture of Large Language Models, by building a framework called LLMSurgeon that can estimate the domain-level distribution of the pretraining corpus. The result is a tool that can diagnose the 'digital DNA' of these models, making it easier to audit their data combination or provenance. This research may have implications for those using LLMs in their IT infrastructure, particularly in terms of understanding and mitigating potential biases or errors.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

If you manage LLM-based systems, here is what to do: review your model's documentation to see if the pretraining data mixture is disclosed, check if your organisation has any auditing or testing procedures in place for LLMs, and consider using a tool like LLMSurgeon to estimate the domain-level distribution of the pretraining corpus. This flags a risk: if your org uses LLMs without understanding their pretraining data mixture, check for potential biases or errors immediately. Not directly applicable to general cloud infrastructure work, but relevant to those working with LLMs.
