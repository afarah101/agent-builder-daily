Stuart Bladon, Brinnae Bent · Academic Institution · 22 May 2026

This research solves the problem of geopolitical bias in language models, finding that it originates in post-training rather than pre-training. The authors built a probe to test seven language model pairs and found that six showed shifts in the direction associated with the country or region of the model developer. The result is a better understanding of where bias in AI models comes from.
