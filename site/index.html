Meriem Mastouri, Emna Ksontini, Amine Barrak·25 Oct 2026

This study maps 14 operational contracts that must hold when running LLM agent containers on Docker, Compose or Kubernetes, and shows that many deployments miss these guarantees, causing state loss and failures. The authors provide a catalogue, detection rules, a fault‑injection tool and a taxonomy of 35 repair patterns to help detect and fix such issues. For most IT infrastructure teams, the findings are highly relevant because they expose hidden dependencies between container lifecycle events and agent state management.

How to apply: If you manage containerised LLM agents, check your platform and framework documentation for the 14 contracts; use the supplied detection rules to scan your deployments for missing guarantees; and apply the 35 repair patterns to fix state‑loss or interruption failures before they hit production.
