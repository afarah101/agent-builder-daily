TSCG: Deterministic Tool-Schema Compilation for Agentic LLM Deployments

Furkan Sakizli · Academic Institution · 04 May 2026

This research solves the problem of tool-schema compilation for large language models by building a deterministic tool-schema compiler called TSCG, which converts JSON schemas into token-efficient structured text, resulting in improved tool-use success rates. TSCG achieves this without requiring model access, fine-tuning, or runtime search. This innovation has the potential to enhance the performance of production agent frameworks.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

If you manage large language model deployments, here is what to do: review your current tool-schema compilation process, consider integrating TSCG to improve token efficiency, and assess the potential benefits for your production catalog sizes.
