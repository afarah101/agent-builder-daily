When Derived Measurements Mislead: Quantifying and Mitigating LLM Over-Trust with Privileged-Modality Reliability Evidence

Zongheng Guo, Tao Chen, Tianli Li, Mingzhe Cui · Academic Institution · 30 Jul 2026

This research solves the problem of over-trusting derived measurements in large language models, where the team built a system to quantify and mitigate this issue using physiological sensing as a case study, resulting in a better understanding of how to avoid assigning incorrect epistemic status to derived measurements. The study used ECG and PPG-derived rhythm to test acceptance and rejection of reliable and contradictory data. This research does not directly affect cloud infrastructure management work.

AIGen: Automating AI Bill of Materials Generation Through Hybrid MLOps Integration

Federica Pepe, Daniele Bifolco, Costantino Martignetti, Aureliano D'Amici · Academic Institution · 29 Jul 2026

This research solves the problem of manually documenting AI system components by building AIGen, a tool that automates the generation of AI Bills of Materials. AIGen produces machine-readable inventories of AI system artifacts, making it easier to track and manage AI deployments. The result is a more streamlined and compliant AI development process.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

If you manage AI or machine learning infrastructure, here is what to do: review your current documentation process, consider integrating AIGen to automate AIBoM generation, and ensure compliance with the SPDX 3.0 standard. This flags a risk: if your org uses AI systems without proper documentation, check your audit and compliance processes immediately.
