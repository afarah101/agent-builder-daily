Characterizing Vision-Language-Action Models across XPUs: Constraints and Acceleration for On-Robot Deployment

Kaijun Zhou, Qiwei Chen, Da Peng, Zhiyang Li · Academic Institution · 2026-04-27

The problem of deploying Vision-Language-Action models on robots is bottlenecked by real-time inference under tight cost and energy budgets. To address this, the authors present a systematic analysis for low-cost VLA deployment via model-hardware co-characterization, evaluating model-hardware pairs under cost, energy, and time constraints. The outcome shows that right-sized edge devices can be more effective than desktop-grade GPUs for on-robot deployment.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

AI practitioners can apply the lessons from this study by considering the trade-offs between different edge accelerators and selecting the most suitable one for their specific use case. By characterizing the constraints and opportunities of their model-hardware pairs, practitioners can optimize their deployments for better performance and efficiency.

BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY

The research has a clear product opportunity in the field of robotics, particularly in areas where real-time inference and low-cost deployment are crucial, such as warehouse automation or service robotics. Companies can leverage the findings of this study to develop optimized Vision-Language-Action models and edge devices for on-robot deployment, creating new business opportunities in the robotics and automation sectors.
