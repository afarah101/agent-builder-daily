William Oliveira · Academic Institution · 2026-04-27

The case study explores the engineering challenges of integrating Small Language Models (SLMs) into a mobile application, specifically a word-guessing game called Palabrita. The authors employed a longitudinal practitioner case study approach, documenting the integration of two SLMs, Gemma 4 E2B and Qwen3 0.6B, over a 5-day development sprint.
