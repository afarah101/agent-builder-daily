OpenAI models, Codex, and Managed Agents come to AWS

OpenAI has made its models, including GPT, Codex, and Managed Agents, available on Amazon Web Services (AWS), allowing enterprises to build and deploy secure AI solutions within their existing AWS environments. This integration enables companies to leverage OpenAI's advanced AI capabilities while maintaining control and security over their data and applications.

HOW TO USE THIS

Enterprises can immediately start using OpenAI models on AWS to develop and deploy customized AI solutions, such as chatbots, language translators, and content generators, to enhance their customer experiences and improve operational efficiency. AI consultants can also utilize this integration to provide their clients with more comprehensive and secure AI solutions, leveraging the combined strengths of OpenAI and AWS.
