The Rollout Infrastructure Tax in Coding-Agent Reinforcement Learning

Daniel Thi Graviet, Lovre Pesut, Ivan Dagelic, Vedran Jukic · Academic Institution · 01 Jul 2026

This research solves the problem of inefficient infrastructure use in coding-agent reinforcement learning by measuring infrastructure overhead, the team built a comparative study of four execution substrates, and the result showed significant variation in performance. The study found up to 110 times variation in cold-start latency and a 1.8 times spread in processing time. This research aims to reveal practical efficiency gains for post-training reinforcement learning.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

If you manage cloud infrastructure for reinforcement learning workloads, here is what to do: review your current execution substrates, consider alternatives such as Kubernetes-orchestrated containers or cloud virtual machines, and optimize your infrastructure to minimize cold-start latency and processing time. This flags a risk: if your org uses single containers or hosted sandboxes, check your infrastructure overhead immediately. Not directly applicable to general IT infrastructure work outside of reinforcement learning.
