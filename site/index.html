Benchmarking Source-Sensitive Reasoning in Turkish: Humans and LLMs under Evidential Trust Manipulation

Sercan Karakaş, Yusuf Şimşek · Academic Institution · 2026-04-27

This study investigates how source trustworthiness affects Turkish evidential morphology and whether large language models (LLMs) can track this sensitivity. The researchers used a controlled cloze context experiment to manipulate the perceived reliability of the information source and measured the effect on the use of -DI and -mIs suffixes. The results show a robust trust effect, with High-Trust contexts yielding relatively more -DI and Low-Trust contexts yielding relatively more -mIs.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

To apply this research in practice, AI practitioners can use the findings to improve the performance of LLMs in tasks that require understanding of evidential morphology, such as text generation and language translation.
