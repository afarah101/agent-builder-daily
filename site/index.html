Aaryan Shah, Andrew Hines, Alexia Downs, Denis Bajet

📍 Academic Institution

SUMMARY

The study addresses the need for efficient evaluation methodologies for clinical AI documentation systems, proposing a case-specific, clinician-authored rubric approach. This approach leverages large language models (LLMs) to generate rubrics that approximate clinician agreement, aiming to reduce the time and cost associated with expert review. The methodology was tested across 823 clinical cases, demonstrating its potential for iterative deployment and safe implementation.

HOW TO APPLY THIS

AI practitioners building clinical workflows can apply this methodology by engaging clinicians in the development of case-specific rubrics and leveraging LLMs to scale the evaluation process. 📍 Academic Institution

SUMMARY

📍 Academic Institution

SUMMARY

📍 MIT

SUMMARY

📍 Academic Institution

SUMMARY
