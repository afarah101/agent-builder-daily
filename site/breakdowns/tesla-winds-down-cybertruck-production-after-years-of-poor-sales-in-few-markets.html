Summary

Tesla announced it will cease production of the Cybertruck after limited sales in select markets. The company cited persistent market challenges, low demand, and supply chain constraints as reasons for the shutdown. The decision follows years of underwhelming sales figures, with the vehicle only sold in a handful of regions and failing to meet production targets.
