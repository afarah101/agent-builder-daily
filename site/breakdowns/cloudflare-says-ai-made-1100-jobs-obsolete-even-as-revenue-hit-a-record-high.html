Implement a chatbot or virtual assistant to automate routine IT support tasks, such as password resets or basic troubleshooting. This can be achieved in under 30 days and can provide a quick win to demonstrate the value of AI in IT operations.

Develop a strategic plan to upskill your IT staff to work effectively with AI tools and systems.
