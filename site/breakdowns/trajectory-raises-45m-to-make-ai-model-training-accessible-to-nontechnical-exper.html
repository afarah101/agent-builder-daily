Summary

Trajectory, a startup focused on AI model maintenance, closed a $45 million Series A round led by prominent venture capital firms. The service is positioned for specialized domains such as law and finance, where domain expertise is critical and model drift can have high stakes. Trajectory’s solution promises a cheaper, faster alternative to traditional fine‑tuning practices that typically involve manual data labeling, model retraining, and deployment cycles.

For enterprise IT teams, this development signals a shift toward more automated, domain‑specific model maintenance. The platform’s emphasis on continual learning aligns with industry trends toward model governance, drift detection, and compliance monitoring. By integrating Trajectory’s API or UI into existing pipelines, teams can add a feedback loop that automatically updates models when new data arrives or when performance metrics fall below thresholds.
