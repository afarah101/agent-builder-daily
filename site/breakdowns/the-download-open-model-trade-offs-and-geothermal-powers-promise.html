Regulatory frameworks like the UK GDPR now extend to AI‑generated data, requiring transparency and accountability in how models are trained and deployed. Failure to address these concerns could expose organizations to legal liability, reputational damage, and operational disruption. In short, Z.ai’s model represents a new vector for cyberattacks that demands proactive monitoring, updated detection capabilities, and a clear strategy for integrating AI threat intelligence into existing security operations.
