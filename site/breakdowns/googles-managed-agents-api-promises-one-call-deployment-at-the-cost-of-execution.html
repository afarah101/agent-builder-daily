Detailed Summary

Google has introduced Managed Agents in its Gemini API, allowing for one-call deployment of agents. This service reduces the time and effort required for deployment, from weeks to a single API call. However, this convenience comes at the cost of control over the execution layer. For enterprise IT teams, this means that fine-grained control over agent deployments may be limited, which could be a concern if they have specific customizations or sandboxing requirements.
