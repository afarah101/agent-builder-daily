It also removes the administrative overhead of onboarding a new vendor, streamlining the procurement cycle and reducing the risk of duplicate contracts or fragmented spend visibility.

In summary, the ability to pay for Hugging Face Hub through GCP billing is a practical step that reduces procurement overhead, improves cost visibility, and aligns AI services with existing cloud governance frameworks.
