How to apply

Evaluate

- Inventory existing document repositories, file formats, and metadata standards. - Assess current data security controls, encryption at rest and in transit, and access governance. - Map legal review workflows to identify bottlenecks, cycle times, and manual touchpoints. - Verify compliance posture: UK GDPR data residency, lawful basis for processing, and data subject rights mechanisms. - Benchmark current throughput: number of documents reviewed per day, average review time, and error rates.

Propose

- Quantify savings: estimate reduction in attorney hours (e.g., 30% faster reviews) and associated cost per hour. - Include risk mitigation metrics: lower error rates, improved audit trail completeness. - Present a pilot ROI: 6‑month pilot with 10% of deals, track time saved, and compare against baseline. - Highlight strategic alignment: faster deal cycles, improved compliance, and competitive differentiation.

Tools to consider

- OpenAI GPT‑4 or GPT‑4o with fine‑tuning for legal terminology. - Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service for secure, on‑prem or private‑cloud deployment. - Google Cloud Vertex AI for managed model hosting and integration with Google Workspace. - IBM Watson Discovery for document parsing and classification. - Elastic Stack (Elasticsearch, Kibana) for indexing, searching, and visualizing AI outputs. - ServiceNow or Freshservice for workflow orchestration and audit logging.

- Data privacy: ensure data does not leave the controlled environment; implement data residency controls. - Model drift: monitor performance over time; establish retraining cadence. - Explainability: provide audit logs of model decisions to satisfy legal and compliance reviews. - Integration complexity: potential API mismatches with legacy document systems. - Vendor lock‑in: evaluate portability of models and data between platforms.
