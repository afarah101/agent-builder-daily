Organizations must demonstrate explainability, auditability, and human oversight for agentic systems that influence critical business processes. Vendors are responding by adding governance frameworks, role‑based access controls, and compliance‑ready SDKs. The trend is likely to accelerate as more firms adopt multi‑cloud AI orchestration platforms that can coordinate agentic workflows across SaaS stacks.

Overall, the report signals that agentic AI is no longer a niche experiment; it is becoming a core enabler for digital transformation in EU enterprises. IT leaders need to assess readiness, build a clear ROI model, and align with compliance mandates to capture the projected productivity gains.
