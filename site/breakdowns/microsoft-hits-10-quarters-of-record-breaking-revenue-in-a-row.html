Evaluate

• Inventory all Azure subscriptions and Microsoft 365 tenant configurations. • Identify AI‑enabled services (Azure OpenAI, Copilot, Cognitive Services) currently in use or provisioned. • Review existing licensing agreements for AI add‑ons, volume discounts, and usage caps. • Map current usage patterns against cost allocation tags to detect hidden or unbudgeted AI consumption.

Propose
