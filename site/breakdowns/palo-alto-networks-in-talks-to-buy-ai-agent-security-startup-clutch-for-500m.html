The acquisition is aimed at strengthening Palo Alto’s defensive posture against the growing threat of AI‑driven cyberattacks, where malicious actors use generative AI to craft phishing messages, automate credential stuffing, or orchestrate sophisticated ransomware campaigns. By integrating Clutch’s technology, Palo Alto plans to embed AI‑agent monitoring directly into its Cortex XDR platform, potentially adding real‑time behavioral analytics, policy enforcement, and automated containment for AI agents.
