AskALCF: A Self-Improving Agentic System for Intelligent HPC User Support

Jingyan Jiang, Akhilesh Bondapalli, Krishna Rauniyar, Murat Keceli · Academic Institution · 2026-11-11 · Generated 14 Nov 2026, 13:19

Summary

AskALCF is a self‑improving AI agent designed to enhance high‑performance computing (HPC) user support by continuously monitoring and diagnosing failures in production interactions. The study deployed AskALCF in a large academic HPC environment, capturing thousands of support tickets and chat logs. The system categorizes failures into five root‑cause buckets: missing knowledge, retrieval errors, prompting deficiencies, model inaccuracies, and tool integration faults. By automatically logging these categories and feeding them back into the training pipeline, AskALCF reduced unexplained failures from 41.4% to 14.2% over a six‑month period. Expert agreement on corrective actions improved from 68% to 92%, indicating higher confidence in the system’s diagnostics. The research demonstrates that a feedback loop—where the AI learns from its own error patterns—can significantly reduce the maintenance burden on support staff and improve service reliability without constant manual model retraining.

For enterprise IT, the implications are substantial. HPC and other mission‑critical systems rely on rapid, accurate support to minimize downtime. AskALCF’s approach allows organizations to maintain a high‑quality knowledge base with minimal human intervention, freeing support engineers to focus on complex issues. The reduction in unexplained failures translates directly to fewer escalations, lower incident response times, and improved user satisfaction scores. Moreover, the system’s transparent failure taxonomy provides a clear audit trail, aiding compliance with internal change‑management policies and external regulatory requirements such as ISO/IEC 27001. The ability to quantify failure types also supports capacity planning and SLA management, enabling IT leaders to set realistic performance targets and measure progress against them.

In practice, enterprises can adopt AskALCF’s methodology to build a self‑healing support ecosystem. By integrating continuous failure monitoring, automated root‑cause classification, and iterative model refinement, organizations can achieve a more resilient support stack, reduce operational costs, and maintain a competitive edge in service delivery. The case study validates that a data‑driven, self‑improving AI framework is not only feasible but also delivers measurable business value in complex, high‑stakes computing environments.
