Google DeepMind has launched AlphaGenome 2, a next‑generation DNA sequence model that predicts the impact of genetic variants on gene expression at single‑cell resolution. The model is exposed through a RESTful API and builds on the success of its predecessor by incorporating a larger training corpus, improved transformer architecture, and cell‑type‑specific attention mechanisms. AlphaGenome 2 can output variant effect scores for thousands of human cell types, enabling researchers to map regulatory perturbations to disease phenotypes with higher precision.

For enterprise IT teams, the release is significant because it introduces a new, high‑performance bioinformatics service that can be integrated into existing data pipelines. Organizations that already ingest genomic data—such as life‑science companies, clinical labs, or security teams that analyze biological threat vectors—can now query variant effects in near real‑time without maintaining large local models.
