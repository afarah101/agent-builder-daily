Detailed Summary

Microsoft has been prioritizing water stewardship since the early 2000s when it started building its datacenters. The company has been working to reduce its water intensity, which is the amount of water used per unit of power consumed, while still scaling to meet the growing demand for cloud and AI services. This is important for enterprise IT teams because as cloud services continue to grow, communities are becoming increasingly concerned about the impact of datacenters on local resources, particularly water. Microsoft's approach to datacenter sustainability involves using advanced technologies such as air-side and water-side economization, which use outside air or water to cool datacenters instead of traditional mechanical cooling systems. The company has also been working with vendors to develop more efficient cooling systems and has implemented water recycling and reuse programs. The broader industry implications of Microsoft's efforts are significant, as the company is helping to drive the development of more sustainable datacenter technologies and practices. Other vendors, such as Google and Amazon, are also working to reduce their water intensity and improve the sustainability of their datacenters. As the demand for cloud services continues to grow, it is likely that water stewardship will become an increasingly important issue for enterprise IT teams.
