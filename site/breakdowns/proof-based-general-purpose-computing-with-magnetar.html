Microsoft Research has released Magnetar, a general‑purpose zero‑knowledge proof (ZKP) library that can be integrated into mainstream programming languages. Magnetar enables developers to generate cryptographic proofs that a computation was performed correctly without revealing the underlying data or re‑executing the logic. The library supports standard languages such as C#, Java, and Python, and claims up to a 10× performance improvement over prior ZKP frameworks. Importantly, Magnetar’s construction is quantum‑resistant, leveraging lattice‑based primitives that are believed to withstand attacks from quantum computers.
