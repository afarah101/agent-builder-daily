IT engineer application guide

Evaluate

Before implementing generative AI, audit your current workflows and identify areas where AI can bring significant benefits. Assess the current level of automation, the types of tasks being performed, and the potential for AI to improve efficiency and quality.

Propose

Build a business case for leadership by highlighting the potential benefits of generative AI, such as increased efficiency, improved quality, and enhanced customer experience. Use metrics such as cost savings, productivity gains, and customer satisfaction to make a compelling case.

Tools to consider

Consider platforms such as Google Cloud AI Platform, Microsoft Azure Machine Learning, and IBM Watson Studio, which offer a range of tools and services for building and deploying AI models.

Risks to flag

Flag technical risks such as data quality issues, model bias, and integration challenges. Also, consider compliance risks such as UK GDPR, and operational risks such as job displacement and skills gaps.
