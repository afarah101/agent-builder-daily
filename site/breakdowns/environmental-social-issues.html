Failure to integrate these metrics can result in reputational risk, loss of investment, and potential non‑compliance with emerging regulations such as the UK’s Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive.

In summary, the Deloitte report signals that environmental and social considerations are no longer peripheral; they are core drivers of IT strategy, vendor selection, and operational efficiency. IT leaders must now quantify ESG impact, embed it into budgeting, and leverage cloud and analytics platforms that provide measurable sustainability outcomes.
