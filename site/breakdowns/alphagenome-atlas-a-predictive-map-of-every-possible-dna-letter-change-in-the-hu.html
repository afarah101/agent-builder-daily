SECTION_SUMMARY AlphaGenome Atlas, released by Google DeepMind, delivers a comprehensive predictive map of the molecular effects of 9 billion single‑letter DNA variants across the human genome. The atlas is built on a massive integration of genomic, epigenomic, and functional datasets, and uses deep learning to predict variant impact on protein structure, gene expression, and disease risk. For enterprise IT teams, this represents a new source of high‑value data that must be ingested, stored, and analyzed at scale. Industry implications include accelerated precision‑medicine pipelines, new regulatory requirements for genomic data handling, and a shift toward AI‑driven variant interpretation as a core service offering.
