Microsoft has released research emphasizing the importance of execution and scalability in artificial intelligence adoption, rather than experimentation. This matters for enterprise IT teams because it emphasizes the need to assess the current state of AI adoption across business units and focus on scalability and delivery of AI outcomes. Enterprise IT teams should consider the scalability and delivery of AI outcomes in their organization and assess the current state of AI adoption across their business units.

This will require a thorough assessment of the current state of AI adoption, as well as an evaluation of the technical, compliance, and operational risks associated with AI initiatives.
