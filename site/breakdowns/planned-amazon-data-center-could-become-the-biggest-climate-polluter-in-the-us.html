Build a business case for investing in sustainable IT infrastructure by highlighting the potential cost savings and reputational benefits. Use metrics such as carbon emissions per user or energy consumption per server to make your case.

Consider working with cloud providers that prioritize sustainability, such as Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, or IBM Cloud. Also, look into tools like Carbon Footprint, Sustainability Manager, or Energy Management Software to help track and reduce your carbon footprint.
