The rollout also pressures competitors to accelerate their own multimodal offerings, potentially leading to a wave of AI‑powered support portals that can handle text, image, and video queries in a single interface. Enterprises that adopt GPT‑6 early may gain a competitive edge in employee productivity and customer support, but they must also navigate the technical debt of integrating a rapidly evolving AI platform with legacy systems.
