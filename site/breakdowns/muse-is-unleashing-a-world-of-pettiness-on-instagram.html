IT security teams must now monitor for unauthorized automation, enforce policy compliance, and ensure that any AI‑enabled social‑media workflows are vetted against platform rules. Failure to do so could result in account suspension, reputational damage, or legal exposure, especially under data‑protection regimes such as UK GDPR where automated decisions affecting individuals must be transparent and auditable. Enterprises should therefore review their social‑media governance frameworks, update monitoring capabilities, and consider the technical and compliance risks of adopting AI assistants like Muse.
